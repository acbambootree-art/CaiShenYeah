# PRODUCT.md — CaiShenYeah, The Digital Temple of Fortune

## What it is
A digital temple for Singapore Pools 4D culture. Not a prediction service: walk-forward
backtests prove the draw is uniform and no model beats chance, and the site says so
plainly. The product is ritual, culture, and honest data: kau chim fortune sticks,
dream number interpretation, incense streaks, a number checker over 20+ years of real
draw history, and transparent model-vs-chance statistics.

## Register
brand — the temple experience IS the product. Utility surfaces (number checker,
statistics) live inside it as product-register halls.

## Users
- Singapore/Malaysia 4D punters, mostly on phones, checking after Wed/Sat/Sun draws.
- Chinese-diaspora users who can't visit a physical temple but grew up with the rituals.
- Family WhatsApp/Telegram groups where numbers and blessings are shared socially.

## Jobs to be done
1. Daily ritual: light incense, shake the sticks, get today's blessed number. (retention)
2. After-draw: did my numbers strike? What were the results? (utility, 3× a week)
3. Dream last night → what numbers? (culture, high emotional moment)
4. Occasional: browse patterns, verify the temple is honest. (trust)

## Brand voice
Reverent but winking. Gold-on-lacquer-black temple aesthetic: joss sticks, red
cylinders, lanterns. Singlish warmth allowed ("Make You Huat"). Three physical-object
words: lacquered, incense-lit, gilded.

## Strategic principles
- Honesty is sacred: every number shown carries the "same 1-in-10,000 chance" line.
  Model Performance and Draw Fairness panels are permanent. Never sell accuracy.
- Ritual over prediction: features add ceremony and culture, never pseudo-science.
- Responsible play: NCPG helpline in the footer, "play responsibly" on share cards.
- Static-first: no backend until traction demands it; all data from 4d_results.json.

## Anti-references
- Gambling-tipster sites promising "sure win" numbers.
- Generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics for the ritual surfaces.
- Anything that buries or softens the honesty panels.
